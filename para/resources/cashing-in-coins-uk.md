# Cashing In Coins (UK)

## Options

**Coinstar (easiest)** — machines in Tesco/Sainsbury's
- 9.9% fee for cash payout
- No fee if you take a gift card (Tesco, Amazon etc.) or donate to charity
- Nearest to Barnet: Tesco Finchley or Brent Cross — verify at coinstar.co.uk
- Near Moorgate: Tesco Express London Wall, Sainsbury's near Liverpool Street

**Roll coins yourself (free)** — bag into standard bank bags (e.g. 20x £1 = £20 bag) and deposit into your own account. Free from most branches. Tedious but no cost.

**Cash4Coins** — post or request collection, paid to bank account. Good for large amounts. Primarily marketed for foreign coins but handles UK too.

**Not viable:** Post Office and bank counters do not accept loose UK coins.

## Recommendation
Coinstar at Tesco Finchley. Take a Tesco voucher instead of cash to avoid the 9.9% fee.
