# Blood Tests: Health Check & Vitamin/Nutrient Monitoring (UK)

*Researched: 2026-03-23*

## NHS
- Free via GP referral, but only for clinically indicated tests (symptoms/conditions required)
- Results take 1-3 weeks; no routine vitamin/mineral screening for healthy adults
- Golders Green Community Diagnostic Centre (Finchley Road) handles local NHS blood tests

## Private — in-clinic venous (most accurate)
- **Mayfield Clinic** (Barnet & Edgeware) — local, 1,500+ tests, named GP interprets results
- **Private Blood Tests London** (South Ken) — £50 phlebotomy + £29+ per test, 1,000+ tests, same/next-day appointments
- **Wellness London** (Marylebone) — tiered packages: Essential £195 (30 markers) → Core £295 (50 markers inc. vit D, ferritin) → Complete £395 (65 markers inc. B12, folate)
- **Health Screening Clinic** (South Ken) — Full Body MOT from £349 (50 markers, 1-day results)
- **DocTap** (9 London clinics) — on-site labs, results in ~15 mins, £75-189

## Private — at-home finger prick kits (convenient but less accurate)
- **Thriva** — Vitamins test £82 (D, B9, B12), results in 48hrs
- **Vitall** — Vitamins & Minerals Complete £99, Full Blood Count £79
- **The Test Clinic** — Complete Vitamin & Mineral £150 (25+ markers)
- Caveat: finger prick can vary 5-15% from venous for hormones/lipids; good for screening, confirm abnormals with venous draw

## Recommendation
Mayfield Clinic in Barnet is the most convenient for comprehensive venous testing. For a first health MOT, Wellness London Core (£295, 50 markers) or Health Screening Clinic Basic (£349) gives solid coverage.
